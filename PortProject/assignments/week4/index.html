This is Fake Pizza company

  • Servings: 4 - 6 portions
  • Prep Time: 15 mins
  • Calories: ~350 kcal / serving
Name Amount
Grands biscuit dough 16.3 ounce can (8 count)
Melted butter ¼ cup
Parmesan cheese, grated 2 teaspoons
Italian seasoning 4 teaspoons (divided)
Cream cheese, softened 8 ounces
Ricotta cheese 1 cup
Marinara sauce (or pizza sauce) 1 cup
Mozzarella cheese, grated 1 ½ cups
Pepperoni, quartered 8 slices

Instructions

1.Ingredients Prep: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and gather all ingredients (marinara sauce, parmesan cheese, ricotta cheese, cream cheese, canned biscuit dough, pepperoni, mozzarella cheese, butter, and Italian seasoning).
2.Coat the Dough: Cut each biscuit in half, roll them into small dough balls, and toss them in melted butter mixed with parmesan cheese and Italian seasoning.
3.Arrange the Crust: Line the coated dough balls side-by-side around the outer edge of a greased cast-iron skillet.
4.Add the Cheese Base: Beat the cream cheese, ricotta cheese, and 2 teaspoons of Italian seasoning together, then spread the mixture evenly in the center of the skillet.Add the Cheese Base: Beat the cream cheese, ricotta cheese, and 2 teaspoons of Italian seasoning together, then spread the mixture evenly in the center of the skillet.
5.Layer the Sauce: Spread the marinara sauce directly over the cream cheese layer.
6.Top with Mozzarella: Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese generously over the sauce, followed by a pinch of Italian seasoning.
7.Add Pepperoni: Arrange the sliced pepperoni pieces evenly over the cheese.
8.Bake: Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes until the biscuit crust turns golden brown and the cheese is fully melted and bubbly.
9.Serve: Garnish with fresh chopped parsley if desired, grab a warm biscuit from the edge, dip, and enjoy NAJA!
